    Sources & assumptions

    What this guide takes from the developer's documents, and what it fills in.

    • DG West brochure (Reportage Group) — developer brochure Plans for floors 5–24 and the 25th floor; unit areas (studio 41 m²; one-bed 65 + 5 m²; two-bed 85 + 5 m²) and every room size shown here.
    • DG West factsheet 25 floors, 200 units (80 studios, 80 one-bed, 40 two-bed), 1,335.5 m² plot on Sports Road, amenity list.
    • Unsplash All photographs are real photos used under the Unsplash License. The tower is still under construction, so none of them show DG West: the background is Nairobi's skyline and the interiors are comparable homes.

      • Filled in by this guide

    • Plans are redrawn from the printed room sizes; the brochure itself says its drawings are approximate and not to scale. Furniture follows the furniture drawn on the brochure plans.
    • Storey heights aren't published. The elevation and the heights quoted assume 3.2 m floor-to-floor on residential levels.
    • Prices aren't published in either document, so none are shown.
